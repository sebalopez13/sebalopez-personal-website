The rhythm

July 2024

Everybody is dancing at a different bit. The chaos created keeps pushing us all like waves crushing in the top of the ocean.

Then you find this space, this line in the wave that carries you with the perfect balance, rhythm, shape, which makes you shine and execute a perfect jump that was your dream. But then you crush and you fucked another drop that was trying to do something as beautiful as your jump.

◇

When we say follow your rhythm, listen to your heart, follow your gut, it's just mindless care for all this. Just care for what your next jump will be. What it will accomplish. Define the goal, and create the chaos you need to perform that.

Find that thin line of marvelous coincidence when the chaos forms in a perfect structure that holds your dreams to happen.
